Elder Bosses - Promised Consort battle music

"Blackmoor Colossus" / "Colossal Boss Battle Theme"

The author-provided "Blackmoor Colossus Loop (No Vocals).wav" and
"Blackmoor Colossus Loop.wav" were normalized to -20 LUFS and encoded as stereo
Ogg Vorbis for the first and second combat phases. Eight-millisecond boundary
fades remove loop clicks. Runtime fades are added.

Elder Bosses - Malenia battle music

"Invasion" by Sascha Ende (2013)
Source: https://ende.app/en/song/206-invasion
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Author's licensing terms: https://ende.app/en/standard-license

Adaptations: excerpts at 52-120 and 136-204 seconds, two-second cyclic
crossfades, eight-millisecond seam ramps, loudness normalization to -21 / -19 LUFS and stereo Ogg Vorbis
encoding. Each resulting loop lasts 66 seconds. Runtime phase crossfades
and parry ducking are applied separately. No author endorsement is implied.
The mod's All Rights Reserved notice does not apply to these music files;
these adaptations retain the CC BY 4.0 license. No Elden Ring audio sampled.

Files: assets/elder_bosses/sounds/music/malenia/phase_one.ogg
       assets/elder_bosses/sounds/music/malenia/phase_two.ogg
